Three things landed this time, and they are the ones that make it start to feel like the
game and not a walking simulator: a real inventory, real combat, and the first player versus
player.

![The inventory open with a dagger tooltip, next to my character and a target window]({{ "/assets/img/hero-part6.png" | relative_url }})

## Items

The inventory window is now the real one. It is built from the original Lineage 2 interface
art, the same window with the paperdoll on top and the item grid below, the two tabs, the
adena count and the weight bar. I keep it as an editable scene so I can move things around in
the editor instead of guessing numbers in code.

The harder part was the items themselves. When you enter the world the server sends you a
list of everything you carry, but each item is just numbers: an id, a count, whether it is
equipped, an enchant level. To turn that into something you can read I had to go back into the
client files. The names, the descriptions, the icons and the stats all live in encrypted
`.dat` files, so I decrypted them and pulled them into small tables the game can read.

Now every item has its real name, its real icon, and a tooltip with the real stats. For a
weapon that is P. Atk, M. Atk, attack speed, soulshot and spiritshot use, and the weight. For
example the starting dagger, straight out of the decrypted table:

```json
"10": {
  "t": "weapon", "w": 1160, "hand": 1,
  "patk": 5, "matk": 5, "spd": 433,
  "ss": 1, "sps": 1, "wt": 3
}
```

`spd` 433 is the fastest attack speed group in the game, so the tooltip shows "Very Fast",
and `wt` 3 means it is a dagger, so the type line reads "Dagger / One-Handed / No-Grade". That
lines up exactly with what the real client shows for that item.

Equipping works the way it does in L2. You drag an item onto the paperdoll, or right click it,
or double click it, and the character equips it. The important thing is I do not decide that on
my side. I tell the server "use this item", and the server is the one that equips or unequips
it and sends the inventory back changed. The window just draws whatever the server last said I
have.

## Hitting things

My character can fight now. You click a wolf once to target it, and click it again to attack.
The character walks into range on its own, faces the wolf, and swings. The swing is the real
animation for the weapon you hold, so a dagger, a sword and a bow all look different.

Like everything else, the hit is not mine to decide. The server sends an attack packet when
the swing starts, and a separate status update at the moment the hit actually lands, with the
new health. So the swing plays on my side, but the health bar only drops when the server says
the hit connected, and a miss just says miss. I had to line those two up on purpose, otherwise
the numbers and the animation drifted apart. The little combat log in the bottom left is fed
from the same two packets.

When a wolf dies it plays its death animation and drops, and the server cleans up the corpse a
moment later.

## Other players

The last one is the first real PvP. With two clients logged into the same server I can attack
the other player.

A normal click on another player just follows or interacts with them, it does not hit them.
To actually start a fight you hold Ctrl and click, which is the force attack, same as retail.
The moment you hit someone their name turns purple, that is the flag, and it blinks near the
end before it wears off. If you kill someone who is not flagged you go red instead, that is
karma. None of that is on my side either. The server runs all the timers and just tells the
clients "this player is purple now", and the name plate reads the color from that.

## Where this leaves me

So now I can carry real items with real stats, equip them, kill the wolves around town, and
turn on another player. It is still basic melee, no skills yet, no potions, no soulshots
actually firing. That is the next block: skills and the effects that go with them.
